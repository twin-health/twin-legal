Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 · Last Updated: November 6, 2026

1. Who We Are

Twin is an iOS app operated by Yaroslav Pavlovych Rozenko, a sole proprietor registered in Ukraine. We are the controller of the information described in this policy.

For privacy-related questions: twinapp.ai@gmail.com

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, Twin processes information you provide:

  • weight and goals;
  • food logs and nutrition information;
  • food photos submitted for recognition;
  • messages submitted to the AI chat.

This information may reveal details about your health or diet. Twin does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

We also receive technical usage and device information through analytics (see section 5).

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Twin does not have user accounts or its own user database. Your weight, goals, food logs, and history are stored locally on your device, and we do not keep a copy on our servers. Device backups you make through Apple (for example, iCloud Backup) may include this data and are managed by Apple.

If you delete the app, locally stored data is removed from your device. Backups you made through Apple’s services are managed by Apple.

4. AI Processing and International Transfers

Twin uses OpenAI technology for food photo recognition and AI chat. When you use these features, the relevant photo or text is sent to OpenAI, which is located in the United States, for processing. This means your data may be transferred outside your country.

Twin does not save food photos in its own app storage. However, this does not mean that OpenAI or other service providers never process or retain the information. Their handling of submitted data is subject to their own terms and privacy policies. According to OpenAI’s published terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default and may be retained for a limited period for abuse monitoring. These terms are set by OpenAI and may change.

Please do not include other people’s faces, documents, or sensitive information in photos or messages.

5. Analytics

Twin uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how the app is used and to measure app activity. It may collect an app installation identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, approximate location derived from IP address, and in-app events and usage statistics. Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Twin does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

6. Payments

Paid subscriptions are processed through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Twin does not receive your payment card details. We may receive limited subscription status information from Apple to unlock paid features.

7. Legal Bases and Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region with similar laws, we process your information:

  • to provide the app and its features you request (contract);
  • with your consent, including explicit consent for processing data that may relate to your health, which you give when you choose to enter such data or use the AI features. You may withdraw consent at any time by stopping use of the relevant features or deleting your data;
  • for our legitimate interests in understanding and improving the app (analytics), where permitted by law.

8. Retention

Data stored on your device is kept until you delete it or delete the app. Data processed by AI providers is retained according to their policies. Analytics data is retained for the period set in Firebase, up to 14 months.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or export your information, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. To exercise your rights, contact twinapp.ai@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Data Deletion

Twin includes an in-app option to delete user data. Use it to delete the data available for deletion within the app.

Deleting data in Twin does not delete information already processed or retained by third-party providers under their own policies, or information that must be retained for legal reasons. To ask about data held by providers, contact us and we will assist where possible.

11. Children

Twin is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used Twin, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including encryption of data in transit. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Twin’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be available in the app, with an updated “Last Updated” date.
